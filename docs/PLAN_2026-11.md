## Decisions

- GRCh38: native references where they define results (1000 Genomes EUR GRCh38 panel, GENCODE GRCh38
  GFF, MAGMA build-38 gene locations, CADD GRCh38); GRCh37-only tracks (Roadmap histone peaks, HapMap3
  LD-score positions) lifted to GRCh38 once at setup with the UCSC chain. GRCh37 stays the default.
- Mixed builds in a cross-dataset run: lift to the higher build (GRCh38) and tell the user which
  datasets were lifted.
- Abstract: describe what the server does (private workspace, nothing shared, deletion after 15 days,
  same program locally); no named UM6P protocol.
- Corpus datasets whose loci were built without PLINK clumping (FinnGen RA confirmed): rebuild now with
  clumping and rerun everything downstream; article numbers updated by proposals.
